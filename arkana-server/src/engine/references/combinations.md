# Combination matrix — reading cards as a story, not a list

The Oracle never says "card A means…, card B means…". It finds the relationship between cards and tells
the single story they form. **This file holds the RULES; the specific-pair BANK (500+ pairs, all
Major×Major + iconic connections covered) lives in `combinations.json`** — scan it first for a drawn
pair, then fall back to the rules here. All names are
canonical (see `cards/`).

## How to use this file
1. After drawing, scan for any **specific pair/triad** below → use it as the plot seed.
2. If no specific match, apply the **general rules** (numbers, suits, arcana, orientation).
3. Always fold the result into the central message (see `interpretation-engine.md`).

---

## General combination rules

- **Hard → renewal** (e.g. *The Rug Pull* / *Liquidation* → *The Hard Fork* → *Bull Market* or any Ace):
  the deck's signature arc — collapse clears the ground, then rebuild. Never end on the collapse.
- **Two+ hard cards** (*The Rug Pull*, *Liquidation*, *FUD*, *Smart Contract Hack*, *Protocol Collapse*,
  *Night Before Liquidation*, *Impermanent Loss*): a genuine warning — but always name the exit the other
  cards provide.
- **Aces present** (*Genesis Block*, *Genesis Liquidity*, *Verified Contract*, *Genesis Treasury*):
  a real new beginning in that suit's domain (⚡ new venture, 💧 new capital/bond, ⚔ new clarity,
  🪙 new wealth).
- **Tens present** (*Validator Overload*, *Financial Freedom*, *Protocol Collapse*, *Legacy Chain*):
  a cycle completing — fulfillment (upright) or overload/painful end (reversed).
- **Many Courts**: the situation is driven by *people*; name the archetypes, not just outcomes.
- **Repeated number**: amplifies that number's theme — Aces=beginnings, Fives=loss/conflict
  (*Fork War*, *Impermanent Loss*, *Hostile Takeover*, *Crypto Winter*), Sevens=patience/assessment,
  Tens=completion.
- **Major + matching Minor** (e.g. *ATH* + *Whale's Vault*; *The Bull Run* + *Verified*): the archetype is
  concretely manifesting right now in that domain.
- **Two Majors adjacent**: a structural turning point — the user is inside a force larger than a choice.

## Suit-pair tensions (when two suits dominate)
- ⚡ Nodes + 🪙 Assets → build vs. bank: turning momentum into lasting value.
- 💧 Liquidity + ⚔ Protocols → heart vs. head: an emotional decision needing cold logic (or vice versa).
- ⚡ Nodes + ⚔ Protocols → drive vs. risk: ambition that must be secured before it ships.
- 💧 Liquidity + 🪙 Assets → people vs. money: relationships entangled with finances.

## Seeded specific combinations (extend to 300–500 over time)

- **Bull Market + ATH + Mass Adoption** → a full growth arc: you've left the hard period behind, a peak
  result is within reach, and it can end in real recognition. *Risk:* losing your head in success.
- **The Rug Pull + The Hard Fork + Genesis Block** → serious disappointment, then leaving the old chain
  behind, then a chance to rebuild something stronger almost from scratch. The loss is not the end.
- **The Rug Pull + ATH** → euphoric greed at the top; the most dangerous moment to add leverage.
- **FUD + The Oracle** → fear clouds a truth your on-chain read already sees; verify, don't panic-sell.
- **Locked Staking + The Hard Fork** → a forced pause that becomes a genuine, irreversible change of course.
- **Diamond Hands + Crypto Winter (Five of Assets)** → conviction tested by a real downturn; the hold is
  the whole test — but check the shelter (community) glowing nearby.
- **The DYOR Sage + Long-Term HODL (Seven of Assets)** → deep patience rewarded; the harvest is near, wait.
- **Liquidation + Protocol Collapse (Ten of Protocols)** → the bottom of a painful collapse; the only
  direction left is up. Survivor's card.
- **The Merge (Lovers) + Governance Vote (Two of Protocols)** → a values-based choice you keep avoiding;
  the deadlock is self-imposed. Decide.
- **The Smart Contract Architect + Genesis Block (Ace of Nodes)** → the moment to build is now; talent and
  ignition are aligned.
- **Builders DAO (Three of Assets) + Master Validator (King of Nodes)** → a strong team behind a clear
  vision; collaboration is your edge.
- **Too Many Tokens (Seven of Liquidity) + FUD** → too many shiny illusions in a fog; choose nothing until
  the fog lifts.
- **Snapshot + Protocol Collapse reversed** → a hard reckoning survived; review the past honestly and be
  reborn from the fork.
- **Mass Adoption + any Ace** → one cycle completes and immediately seeds the next beginning.

## The output, always
From whatever combination you find, produce ONE narrative with a beginning, a turn, and a direction —
then Oracle Advice, a Warning, and a Final Omen. If you can't say the whole spread in one sentence, you
have not synthesized it yet.
