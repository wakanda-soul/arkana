05 · ORE vault

What you spend builds a staked position

The 34% goes into your own tranche in the Arkana vault, and the vault stakes it in the ORE protocol.

Each day

Your first payment of the day opens it. Later payments that day top it up at no extra rent.

The circle closes

06 · Trust

Nothing is taken on the app's word

  • Payments are verified on-chain. The server checks the signer, the burn, the treasury transfer, the ORE deposit and that each signature is used once.
  • The treasury is signed. Its address carries an offline Ed25519 signature that the app checks before every payment.
  • Seeker perks are real. The Seeker Genesis Token is read from the chain, and your wallet signs in with a free message.
  • The code is open. The vault program and its tests against the real ORE Stake program are public.
