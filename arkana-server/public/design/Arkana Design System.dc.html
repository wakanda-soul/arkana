04 · MOTION
AURA
opacity .35→.85, 7s ease-in-out, infinite. One per screen.
CARD FLOAT
translateY 0→−7px, 5–6s ease-in-out, infinite.
SHUFFLE
3 cards, 900ms loop, offsets ±52px / −34px, ends after 1.5s.
REVEAL
rotateY 90°→0 + scale .92→1, 550ms cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1).
TEXT ENTER
translateY 14px + fade, 500ms, staggered 100ms per block.
SEAL
scale .7→1.08→1, 600ms. Haptic: one medium tick on confirm.
PRESS
scale .94, 90ms in / 140ms out. No ripple — Arkana has no Material ripple.
REDUCED MOTION
All infinite loops stop; reveal becomes a 200ms fade. Ship this.
05 · VOICE
WRITE
“Depth returns before conviction does.”
“That is a pattern, not a thesis.”
“Nothing sealed yet.”
Second person. Present tense. One idea per sentence. Name the cost.
DON'T
“Oops! Something went wrong 😔”
“The stars say SOL is going to 500!”
“Unlock your destiny now!!”
No emoji, no price calls, no urgency, no mystical filler. The app never predicts.
06 · ARTWORK TO COMMISSION
I don't generate imagery. These are the slots where real art is required, with the composition each one needs. Everything else in the app is type and hairlines by design.

1000×1515px, ratio 0.66. Gold line art on near-black, single violet light source, no text baked in. Numeral sits bottom-centre in app, not in the art.
ONBOARDING KEY ART
1080×1350. The oracle figure from your reference, cropped to shoulders, centred, bottom 40% fading to #08070b so the headline sits over it.
APP ICON
432×432 adaptive. One gold diamond on #08070b, violet bloom behind it. Must read at 48dp — no figure, no text.
CARD BACK
Single tile, seamless gold lattice at 25% opacity. Used for every face-down card and the shuffle animation.
